Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

ANMOL STORE ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our customers, sellers, and local service providers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access our multi-vendor marketplace platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect account information (name, email, 10-digit mobile number, delivery address), order details, service booking requirements, and business details for registered merchants (shop name, business category, business address, and contact numbers).

3. Location Data Handling

We only access location data when explicitly permitted by you via the location picker or when you enter your city and PIN code. We do not track background GPS locations.

4. Data Protection

All user passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing. Access to private seller orders and customer information is strictly partitioned and authorized on the backend.
