1. Marketplace Platform

ANMOL STORE is a multi-vendor platform connecting local retailers, service providers, and consumers. Products and on-demand repair services are fulfilled directly by the independent merchant whose digital storefront you purchase from.

2. Free Seller Registration Policy

Seller onboarding and account maintenance are free of mandatory registration charges. Sellers retain independence over their pricing, inventory, and fulfillment operations.

3. Multi-Seller Checkout & Delivery

When items from multiple shops are ordered together, they form distinct fulfillment groups under the order model. Delivery charges are calculated transparently per fulfilling seller.

4. Pre-Owned Vehicles & Direct Inquiries

Vehicle listings are informational and facilitate direct communication between buyer and seller. Physical vehicle condition, paperwork verification, and final payments take place directly between the buyer and the seller.
