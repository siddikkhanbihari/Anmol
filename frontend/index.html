Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ANMOL STORE

Is seller registration really 100% free on ANMOL STORE?
Yes, absolutely! Free account creation for customers and free shop registration for sellers is a core policy. We do not charge any hidden registration or subscription fees.
How are multi-seller orders fulfilled?
When you add products from different local shops to your cart, they are grouped under their respective sellers. Each seller handles their item fulfillment directly, and clear delivery charges are displayed transparently.
How do service bookings and vehicle inquiries work?
Services and vehicles are separate workflows! You can book home visits or repair slots with chosen date/time slots, while vehicle listings allow you to submit direct inquiries and negotiate with local sellers.
What payment methods are supported?
We support Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as online UPI and card payments when configured.
