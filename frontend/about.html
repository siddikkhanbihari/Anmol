Our Mission & Story

Aapki Dukaan, Aapka Business, Aapki Pehchaan

ANMOL STORE is India's dedicated hyperlocal digital marketplace built to empower independent retailers, kirana store owners, and service technicians with their own digital storefront.

The Vision Behind ANMOL STORE

India's local business owners have formed the backbone of neighbourhood communities for generations. However, large closed-loop quick commerce companies have often displaced traditional kirana stores and local merchants by hoarding customer access and taking steep margins.

ANMOL STORE bridges this divide. We provide local shopkeepers with a modern digital storefront where they can list products, set their own prices, and connect directly with their neighbours.

Neighbourhood Fulfillment

Products arrive fast and direct from shops within walking or biking distance.

Distinct Workflows

Specialized shopping cart for goods, slot booking for repairs, and inquiries for vehicles.
